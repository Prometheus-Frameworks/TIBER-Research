**Game:** Minnesota 16, Indianapolis 10, October 25, 2026. Sources read October 26, Toronto date. This is the first comparison under the original return question.

The [Vikings activated Mason October 24](https://www.vikings.com/news/jordan-mason-activated-colts-game-week-7-2026). The [game box](https://www.espn.com/nfl/boxscore/_/gameId/401873014) records **15 carries/68 yards** for Mason and **8/26** for Aaron Jones. Mason had **two targets, no receptions**; Jones one target/one catch for zero yards.

Mason's full-game share is **15 / (15 + 8) = 65.2% of RB carries**, compared with **15/29 = 51.7% of all Minnesota carries**. These denominators describe different questions.

### The missing context behind the 65% headline

The [official Vikings postgame account](https://www.vikings.com/news/colts-week-7-game-observations-4th-quarter-lockdown) reports **Jones left with a knee injury** on his 17-yard fourth-quarter run. It also reports Mason's fourth-quarter total as five carries/25 yards.

These are observed event splits, not proof that the injury caused every later assignment. No return timetable, Week 8 designation or diagnosis beyond the team's knee-injury report is established.

Neither back had a carry starting inside the 10 in the inspected ledger. Mason's Q1 carry that finished at IND 10 **started at IND 12** and does not count. The game therefore does not adjudicate a normal goal-line hierarchy. Two incomplete targets also cannot establish route share, pass-protection assignments or absence of a receiving role.

### Update to the competing explanations

- **Quick recovery of meaningful early-down work:** supported by actual participation and a pre-exit rushing lead.
- **Jones retains the clear rushing lead:** not supported by this game's carry counts, although he started.
- **Gradual return / health-dependent redistribution:** remains relevant. Jones' new injury means the last quarter cannot be treated as a clean test of the healthy rotation.
- **Mason becomes a universal passing-down or goal-line owner:** unresolved; this game supplies insufficient evidence.

**Our interpretation:** Mason's meaningful return is established. A stable 65% healthy backfield role is not.
