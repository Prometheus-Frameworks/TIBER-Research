## Question and evidence cutoff

**Opened October 26, 2026 · America/Toronto.** All four selected Sunday games below are final. Week 7 as a whole is not yet complete.

**When a back leads the carries, does he also retain the receiving and close-range opportunities that support repeatable fantasy production?**

Ryan Heath's [public Fantasy Points Data thread](https://x.com/RyanJ_Heath/status/2114536259742044372) supplied the leads. We independently inspected the game sources below. His weighted opportunities, XFP, route shares and positional ranks remain attributed charting unless separately reproduced. This is an ordinary public football study; it does not activate a governed Research run or alter a model.

### Situational evidence inspected

Use **the line of scrimmage before a valid play**, not where the runner finishes. Nullified plays are excluded from official opportunities but retained separately when relevant. Keep inside-5 and inside-10 samples separate; never add overlapping categories into a touch total.

- **Pittsburgh:** Dowdle's Q2 7:29 carry began at NO 9 and gained five yards to the 4. The [full play log](https://www.espn.com/nfl/playbyplay/_/gameId/401873011) supports Heath's report that this was the sole RB carry from inside the 10. Warren's earlier carries reaching the NO 15/20 are not inside-10 starts. One opportunity is weak evidence of permanent goal-line ownership.
- **Green Bay:** [Q1 11:55 and 11:13](https://www.espn.com/nfl/playbyplay/_/gameId/401873020) show Jacobs carries from DET 6 and DET 3, respectively. These are the two reported inside-10 RB carries. His receiving TD later came from 25 yards and is a different channel.
- **Tampa Bay:** the [play log](https://www.espn.com/nfl/playbyplay/_/gameId/401873018) identifies Irving's inside-5 starts at CAR 4 (Q1 7:22, TD), CAR 4 (Q2 4:41, three-yard gain), and CAR 1 (Q2 3:53, TD). Tucker's Q2 15:00 attempt started at CAR 3 and lost one. This supports **Irving 3 of 4**, rather than inferring ownership solely from his two rushing TDs.
- **Denver:** Coleman's two targets match Harvey's two. Dobbins' one-yard rushing TD is verified; Heath's stronger claim that Dobbins had the only inside-10 carry remains **analyst-reported**, because this pass did not reconstruct Denver's complete situational run ledger.

## Definition checks before comparisons

The inspected Pittsburgh box has 30 pass attempts but **28 credited receiving targets**. Warren's four targets are 13.3% of attempts and **14.3% of credited targets**; Dowdle's six are 20.0% and **21.4%**, respectively. Green Bay has 45 attempts and **42 credited targets**: Jacobs' seven are 15.6% and **16.7%**.

Heath's displayed percentages numerically match the pass-attempt denominators in these games. That does not establish every inclusion rule in his provider. Retain his attributed values and our explicitly defined calculations side by side; do not call a convention difference a role change.

We have not reproduced Heath's weighted-opportunity calculation or XFP model. **Do not invent coefficients or relabel carries + targets as XFP.** Until a reproducible, permitted method is selected, retain carries, targets and situational counts separately.
