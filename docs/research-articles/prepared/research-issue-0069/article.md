## 3. Baseline and alternatives

Our working case combines Hockenson's target-earning role with Detroit's unusually high production allowed to tight ends. The strongest competing explanations are a short, opponent-heavy sample, touchdown volatility, changing defensive health, and Minnesota's limited passing volume and competition for targets.
