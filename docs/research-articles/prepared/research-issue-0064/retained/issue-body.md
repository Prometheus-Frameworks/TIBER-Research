## Corrections and evidence qualifications we should carry forward

1. **Scoring:** McBride's 10 catches/111 yards/TD yield **27.1 full PPR**, versus 17.1 without reception points. The notes cannot be labelled uniformly standard scoring. PPR also does not specify QB TD/turnover rules. Record each calculation's rules.
2. **Target share:** our existing #44/#47 ledgers use credited receiver targets. Several Heath percentages numerically match all pass attempts instead. Both must keep their own denominator; a convention difference is not a role change.
3. **Dropbacks:** the Arizona box records Brissett with **48 attempts and three sacks**. The thread's 48-dropback label is not reconciled with those counts. Do not use it as a full-game route denominator.
4. **Exposure:** active games, healthy/full games, team weeks, byes and within-game injuries differ. Cooper's opener and Mason's post-Jones-exit work need explicit treatment.
5. **Efficiency and production:** retain complete outcomes and distribution, then contextual slices. Do not erase the best/worst plays to manufacture a conclusion, or equate raw YAC with individual creation.
6. **Proprietary measurements:** weighted opportunity, XFP, route/design/coverage grades and ranks remain attributed until a permitted, reproducible measurement and comparable population are established. Basic boxes cannot recreate them.
7. **Causality:** before/after QB, personnel or teammate changes occur alongside opponent, health and game-state changes. Record those competing explanations rather than assign a single cause.
8. **Source consistency:** dynamic summaries can lag weekly rows. Cooper's current official log has 103 Week 6 yards while an older feature headline says 102; the Jets recap also has conflicting combined-catch/QB-yard prose. The cases document the exact choices made. Agreement between two publishers may still share upstream lineage.
