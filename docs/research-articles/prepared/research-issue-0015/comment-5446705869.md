#### Contribution to the R2 contract family

Do not create a separate Fantasy-owned shared player-context packet. The Draft Review/player-evidence work should contribute requirements to the candidate `TiberAgentContextPacketV0`, `ContextCompilationTraceV0`, operation-manifest and offline fixture family owned by this R2 design.

Required application semantics:

1. **Status before story.** Identity, team, decision time and per-domain status precede narrative.
2. **Five structural layers.** `observed | derived | forecast | operator | agent` remain distinct.
3. **Unknown is not zero.** Unavailable/source-required domains carry reason plus null metrics, not false, zero or empty observed sets.
4. **Claim-level lineage.** Preserve original asserter/source, compiler/transformer, observation/as-of time, freshness and authority.
5. **Attached private context.** Operator theses require explicit narrow workspace/league/roster/player scope and cannot contaminate another packet.
6. **Agent non-ingestion.** Scenarios, ranges, recommendations, generated UI fields and agent-fetched sources never become World/Forecast/observed evidence automatically.
7. **Transformation ownership.** A deterministic recast of complete named external inputs is a TIBER transform, not a TIBER projection.
8. **Exact entity binding.** Every player claim binds canonical identity so evidence cannot cross between similarly discussed players.
9. **Agency ceiling.** The packet/operations may prepare or explain a decision but cannot execute fantasy actions.
