**Working expectation, explicitly judgment:** Variable, incremental offensive allocation is better supported than a promised immediate full-time WR role. This is a role expectation, not a catch, yardage or fantasy-point projection.

| Hypothesis | What we will test against Indianapolis |
|---|---|
| **H1 broader integration — unresolved** | Strengthen with greater route access plus repeated ordinary passing participation across possessions/concepts, including untargeted work. A touchdown or selected-touch spike alone is counterevidence to calling it established. |
| **H2 specialist package — remains consistent** | Strengthen if heavy defense continues alongside selective offense. Weaken if meaningful receiving breadth expands beyond selected calls at comparable defensive demands. More package plays alone can still fit H2. |
| **H3 workload/availability — plausible, not isolated** | Compare Brown's actual rotation and Hunter's defense with offense. Brown playing without an offensive increase weakens the narrow “return is sufficient” claim; other constraints can persist. Meyers restrictions would make any expansion conditional. |
| **B1 deployment/fit — unresolved** | Compare actual routes, releases, leverage and timely windows. Coen already prioritized the downfield connection; do not presume that plan absent. Suitable assignments with poor execution/delivery weaken a sole system-mismatch explanation. |
| **B2 execution/selection — unresolved as sole cause** | Inspect catchability, route adjustments and blocks before explaining selection. Reported errors matter; confidence statements do not erase them. Coverage or placement can explain apparent errors; no personality diagnosis. |
| **B3 QB connection/protection — unresolved** | Separate clean and pressured opportunities, window timing and placement. Untargeted does not mean missed read. Adequate protection/delivery with failures elsewhere is the strongest alternative. |
| **B4 competition/personnel — live explanation** | Compare BTJ with Washington/Meyers/Cameron/TEs at stated team passing volume and personnel. Changes in total volume or 11/12 personnel can change raw counts without a receiver winning/losing the same job. |

These explanations can coexist. Brown's progress, Meyers' condition and opponent changes prevent a clean single-cause interpretation.

**BTJ's proposed better environment remains a testable condition set:** usable outside/intermediate/deep assignments, release help where needed, coverage leverage, an on-time accurate delivery and execution that sustains selection. Recovery within Jacksonville could support that mechanism. No destination speculation or isolated coaching-effect claim is added.
