##### V1 — Hunter: catch through converging coverage

[0:37–0:46 in the compilation](https://x.com/kowalski532/status/1973769430867095580) · Jacksonville at San Francisco · September 28, 2025 · Week 4 · Q2 11:35 · 3rd-and-15, JAX 6 · 28-yard completion

**Existing interpretation:** Gus Logue’s Duval 22 review points to the catch at 0:37. The compilation is posted by @kowalski532; the underlying footage carries an NFL Pro watermark.

**Own frame observation:** In the inspected sequence, Hunter travels into the middle of the field, raises his arms for the arriving ball and meets a closing defender. The finish is on the ground with defenders around him. The play ledger records a completed catch whose review was upheld. The sparse frames do not independently establish uninterrupted possession through the ground.

**Can establish:** A concrete NFL catch-point rep in a long-yardage situation, under Coen, with surrounding coverage. This is useful evidence to examine Joe’s ball-skills hypothesis.

**Rival explanations:** Catch-point adjustment and contact tolerance may explain the result; ball placement and the available route window also affect its difficulty. One successful rep cannot rank his strongest trait or establish a contested-catch success rate.

**Record:** Locate #12 before the snap. Record inside/outside and on/off line separately; note release contact, route break, defenders at ball arrival, hand/body adjustment and contact timing. Use the finish/replay to check control. Do not turn the completion into a claim that he separated cleanly.



##### V2 — Hunter: an untargeted seam and the timing of “open”

[0:00–0:11 wide view; approximately 0:14–0:24 end-zone replay](https://x.com/kowalski532/status/1973767110255169890) · Jacksonville at San Francisco · September 28, 2025 · Week 4 · PBP match: Q2 09:19 · 2nd-and-9, JAX 47 · Tuten reception for 8 yards

**Existing interpretation:** Logue identifies Hunter on a seam and interprets the coverage as Cover 4. He also cautions that defenders reacting to the quick throw help produce the later appearance of openness. Those coverage and opportunity judgments remain attributed.

**Own frame observation:** The wide view shows a downfield route continuing while Lawrence sends a shorter pass toward the near sideline. Downfield spacing is different by the time the short completion develops. The clip supplies both the receiving distribution and the quarterback/pocket view; it is more informative than a still showing only the late downfield space.

**Can establish:** An NFL route continuing without a target, and a practical test of whether a window existed while the quarterback could still use it. It does not establish a missed touchdown.

**Rival explanations:** Hunter may offer a usable seam window; alternatively, the apparent opening may become large only after the throw, or the called concept may prioritize a valid shorter answer. A defender following a route does not by itself prove an intentionally designed clear-out.

**Record:** Identify #12 and pause at three moments: before the throwing motion, ball release, then receiver arrival. Mark the nearest underneath/deep defender at each point and whether a viable lane existed before release. Describe defender movements before naming coverage. Record purposeful route continuation; leave designed progression unknown.



##### V3 — Hunter: intermediate/deep route while the tight end gets the ball

[0:00–0:16 wide view; approximately 0:18–0:33 end-zone replay](https://x.com/kowalski532/status/1973767346016956521) · Jacksonville at San Francisco · September 28, 2025 · Week 4 · PBP match: Q2 08:06 · 3rd-and-6, midfield · Strange reception for 11 yards

**Existing interpretation:** Logue describes Hunter running a deep post against Cover 4. This is his route/coverage interpretation, not a verified playbook assignment.

**Own frame observation:** The wide view shows routes continuing downfield while the throw goes to a shorter route on the other side. Defenders maintain depth with downfield receivers during the play. The sampled frames do not securely resolve every jersey, the exact break instruction or the entire potential throwing window.

**Can establish:** Another ordinary NFL passing concept in which a reported Hunter route is untargeted. Use the continuous clip to check the analyst’s identification and whether the route offered a timely option or affected spacing.

**Rival explanations:** A post could create a usable window, occupy deeper coverage for an underneath completion, or appear open after the quarterback has committed elsewhere. An appropriate chain-moving completion can coexist with a deeper option; neither proves a bad quarterback read.

**Record:** Track #12 from alignment through the break before accepting the route label. Record release conditions, stem, break depth, defender leverage and the ball-release moment. If claiming he created room for Strange, name the defender who moved and when; otherwise label that mechanism an inference.



##### V4 — BTJ: the isolated receiver on the other side

[0:00–0:14 wide view; approximately 0:16–0:35 end-zone replay](https://x.com/kowalski532/status/1973766213617713206) · Jacksonville at San Francisco · September 28, 2025 · Week 4 · PBP match: Q2 12:06 · 2nd-and-12, JAX 9 · incomplete to Strange; holding, no play

**Existing interpretation:** Logue identifies BTJ away from the multi-receiver side and interprets Lawrence as choosing that side of the concept. The intended read order is not documented.

**Own frame observation:** The offense is backed up near its own goal line. A receiver is isolated outside on the far side against an off defender, with several eligibles opposite. The quarterback moves from his initial pocket position and the ball goes toward a shorter tight-end route rather than the isolated receiver. This does not visually diagnose BTJ’s 2026 difficulties.

**Can establish:** A historical deployment/quarterback-context example under Coen. It provides a way to test “he was open but unused” without assuming that unused means misused.

**Rival explanations:** A timely isolated window could support a connection/timing explanation. Protection movement, the backed-up field position, opposite-side concept or a late-developing window could also explain the choice. One untargeted rep neither proves system mismatch nor excuses execution elsewhere.

**Record:** Confirm #7, then track his release, leverage and break relative to the quarterback’s movement. Record whether a usable window existed before the pocket changed. Separate off coverage from a press-release win. Keep the penalty snap in the film inventory but exclude it from official route/target-rate arithmetic unless the chosen denominator explicitly includes no-plays.
