## Receiving profile and 2025 alignment follow-up — October 20, 2026

**Working refinement:** Study Hunter as a receiver with several possible jobs. Godwin is a reference for how Coen can create opportunities, not a default player comparison. Ball tracking, catch-point timing, release quickness, spatial awareness and playmaking after the catch deserve separate evaluation. Joe's view that ball skills are Hunter's leading trait is a hypothesis to test, rather than a conclusion inferred from a highlight.

### What the 2025 evidence adds

- **Alignment:** Matt Harmon's public October 19 U.S.-evening post reports **41% outside and 59% slot that day**, in a thread about Hunter's Week 7 Rams performance. This is a single-game report, not his season alignment split. The post does not specify its charting denominator; retain the author's terms rather than relabeling it as a percentage of routes. [Original public post](https://x.com/MattHarmon_BYB/status/1980083435952632162)
- **Participation:** Dwain McFarland's public post reports route participation of **63%, 74%, 74%, 88% and 90% in Weeks 3–7**, respectively. This supports a growing receiving workload before the injury. These are attributed reported rates; we do not possess the underlying route counts or the provider's denominator definition. Participation alone does not identify progression position or execution on untargeted plays. [Original public post](https://x.com/dwainmcfarland/status/1980032585154670861)
- **Target depth:** Pro Football Reference's 2025 advanced receiving table lists **7.8-yard aDOT across 45 targets**. This makes a uniformly vertical description of his targeted work a poor starting assumption. It does not measure the depth of every route, nor establish his alignment. [PFR](https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/H/HuntTr00.htm)
- **The remembered Chiefs catch is real:** The NFL's official description identifies a 44-yard catch over two Kansas City defenders. The accessible description confirms the event; it does not establish the pre-snap alignment. The video player failed, so this is not our own visual assessment of the play. [Official highlight](https://www.nfl.com/videos/can-t-miss-play-travis-hunter-outleaps-a-chief-to-snag-44-yard-deep-ball-from-lawrence)

The strongest historical follow-up is therefore to inspect the growing 2025 role, including the weeks before the Rams spike. Outside alignment, being the on-line X receiver and running a vertical route are distinct facts to chart.

### Skill and scheme hypotheses

Curl frequency alone does not establish a YAC-centered profile: a receiver returning toward the quarterback may have to turn and rebuild momentum. SIS contextualized Colorado's curl concentration partly through pass protection and quick distribution. Its charting put Hunter at 5.0 YAC per reception in 2024; that is context, not a verdict on his open-field ceiling. [SIS original analysis](https://www.sportsinfosolutions.com/2025/04/10/analytics-scouting-report-travis-hunter-wide-receiver/)

Candidate jobs to test include outside isolation, intermediate routes through coverage windows, red-zone separation and catch-point work, and manufactured touches in space. Successful contested catches should be distinguished from consistently creating a favorable throwing window. Route timing, contact tolerance, blocking and performance when the ball goes elsewhere remain part of the assessment.

### Current allocation and development

Keep BTJ on the active roster in the baseline. Cameron's physical contribution is relevant: Jacksonville's draft reporting emphasized his blocking, and subsequent reporting described intelligence, personnel competition and increased two-tight-end usage. Neither source establishes that blocking caused every lost BTJ or Hunter snap. [Jaguars, April 29](https://www.jaguars.com/news/how-wr-josh-cameron-fits-the-jaguars-physical-identity) · [ESPN, October 2](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50079470/jaguars-wr-brian-thomas-jr-josh-cameron)

Defensive value belongs in the allocation model. Healthy cornerback depth restores coaching choices; a high-value defensive role can still compete with receiving work. Coen previously described withholding planned offensive work when other corners were unavailable. [Jaguars, October 4](https://www.jaguars.com/news/top-storylines-from-jaguars-week-4-win-over-bengals-big-time-game-for-travis-hunter)

The rookie-season injury interrupted development. Treat receiving experience and continuity as limited without discounting the intervening offseason or calling him literally a rookie again.
