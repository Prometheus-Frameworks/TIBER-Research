##### 4. What we will call meaningful expansion

**Require evidence of both greater opportunity and greater breadth.** A new offensive-snap high above both disputed Week 6 descriptions is an alert to investigate, not a verdict. Compare routes with a stated team passing denominator, then check whether participation recurs on ordinary possessions, in both halves where opportunities exist, and when the ball goes elsewhere.

We will distinguish:

- **Broader integration signal:** increased route access plus varied, repeated concept participation and useful untargeted assignments, with workload and personnel context recorded.
- **Package expansion:** more selected offensive calls without equivalent evidence of ordinary route breadth. This can be valuable and can be a developmental step, while H2 remains viable.
- **Conditional expansion:** a larger role specifically alongside Meyers' restriction, another receiver's absence or a changed cornerback rotation. Record the change without calling it durable.
- **Efficiency-only result:** more yards or touchdowns on similarly restricted work. This does not establish expanded integration.
- **Unassessable breadth:** counts increase but suitable film/charting is unavailable. Report the measured increase and leave concept/progression claims open.

A low-scoring game with broader work can strengthen H1; an explosive touchdown in a tiny package can leave H1 unresolved. No fantasy-point threshold or unsupported snap projection is introduced.
