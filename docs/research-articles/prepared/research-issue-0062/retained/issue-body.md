**Opened October 26, 2026 · America/Toronto. Player: Omar Cooper Jr., New York Jets rookie WR.**

**Do Cooper's explosive first appearances develop into repeatable route and target access, or is the early yardage substantially ahead of the opportunity volume?** His production counts in full; the question is what can recur when conversion changes.

## Week 7 opportunity and gain distribution

The [official game box](https://www.newyorkjets.com/game-day/2026/reg-week7/dolphins-at-jets/box-score) and [ESPN receiving table](https://www.espn.com/nfl/boxscore/_/gameId/401873015) agree on **5 receptions, 115 yards, 1 TD**. ESPN and the [Jets' player-focused recap](https://www.newyorkjets.com/news/omar-cooper-jr-mason-taylor-3-touchdowns-177-yards-vs-dolphins-10-25-2026) each list **five targets**.

- Target share using the study definition: **5 / 29 credited Jets receiving targets = 17.2%**.
- Share of all pass attempts: **5 / 30 = 16.7%**. Label the denominator; these percentages are not interchangeable.
- Garrett Wilson, Adonai Mitchell, Mason Taylor, Braelon Allen and other receivers participated. Taylor had six targets and Allen seven; this was not a monopoly of team targets.
- The official recap identifies gains of **52 and 60 yards**. Together they supplied **112 / 115 = 97.4%** of Cooper's receiving yards. All five catches and the TD remain in the full result; removing the best plays is not a forecast or a skill adjustment.
- Calculated Week 7 receiving-only scoring: **22.5 full PPR / 20.0 half-PPR / 17.5 without reception credit**, using 0.1/yard and six/TD, no bonuses. This illustrates why the supplied notes should not be labelled uniformly standard scoring.

**Our initial inference:** the explosiveness is real in the recorded outcomes, while a high-volume weekly role remains unestablished. Five targets can produce an excellent game; repeating that score need not be the right persistence test.

The Jets recap has internal editorial inconsistencies: its combined-catch summary says 11 although the two displayed player lines total 10, and one paragraph gives Smith 257 yards versus 287 in both boxes. We use the individual reconciled rows, not those conflicting recap totals.

**Strengthen:** meaningful route and target access recurs across different opponents and normal game situations, with varied receiving opportunities.

**Challenge:** a healthy player remains peripheral in routes/targets and usable production repeatedly depends on isolated gains or teammate absences.

**Unresolved:** limited/inactive appearance, missing route evidence, or too little team opportunity. Do not grade a missing witness as a zero.
