#### Question and scope

How does Miami's passing structure look after the Week 6 bye, with Caleb Douglas returning alongside Chris Bell, Malik Washington and **Kevin Coleman Jr.**? We want to distinguish sustainable target earning, player development and role changes from isolated big plays or touchdowns.

**Pregame snapshot:** October 24, 2026, before Dolphins at Jets, Sunday October 25 at 1 p.m. ET. This issue launches the manual audit, captures the initial evidence and preserves the Week 7 questions. The route/alignment and independent film work below remains open.

Coleman is a fourth focal player, including whether his pre-bye involvement survives Douglas's return. These are research starting points, not a predetermined breakout conclusion.

##### Passing environment

Through five games Miami lists Willis at **73 completions on 130 attempts, 849 passing yards, two passing TDs, four interceptions and 14 sacks**. That is 26 attempts per game, calculated here. The audit must test both the share each receiver earns and the size/quality of the passing opportunity. Greg Dulcich and the backs remain part of that allocation. Do not assume current RB availability from cumulative season totals. [11]



#### Bell: preserve the erased-play evidence accurately

One specific example is corroborated in dated reporting: **Week 4 at Minnesota, Q2 12:40, third-and-4 from Miami's 32**. An apparent 68-yard receiving TD was erased for illegal touching after Bell voluntarily stepped out during the scramble sequence. The account describes a catch followed by substantial running yardage. [12]

This is a lead for studying movement, quarterback connection and open-field ability, alongside the execution error that made the play illegal. It is not 68 official receiving yards or a touchdown to add to his totals. Independent film evaluation has not been completed in this audit.

For each additional reported erased play, retain: game/clock, clip or play ID, official ruling, who committed the foul, whether the foul helped create the opening, intended route versus scramble action, catch location and YAC. We have not yet verified an exhaustive multi-play penalty ledger.
