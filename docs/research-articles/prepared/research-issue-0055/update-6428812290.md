##### What the zero misses

- **First quarter, 3:20, third-and-4 at Tampa Bay's 24:** the NFL game book identifies Hurst as the intended receiver on a deep-right throw and records defensive pass interference against Akayleb Evans. The 21-yard penalty produced a first down at Tampa Bay's 45. It was recorded as a **no play**, not an official target, catch or receiving yards; it produced no conventional fantasy points.
- **Second quarter, 14:18, third-and-goal at Carolina's 4:** Hurst's one official target was incomplete. The Buccaneers' game recap describes a shallow out with the pass **deflected at the line by Bobby Okereke**, after which Tampa Bay kicked a field goal. This is not evidence of a Hurst drop. [NFL game book](https://static.www.nfl.com/image/upload/v1793014727/gamecenter/aad59a67-4feb-11f1-abca-2c54536568a9.pdf) · [team recap](https://www.buccaneers.com/news/bucs-panthers-week-7-recap-postgame-report-2026)

**Source reconciliation:** the game book and [ESPN play-by-play](https://www.espn.com/nfl/playbyplay/_/gameId/401873018) identify Hurst on the 21-yard interference play, while the Buccaneers' narrative recap names Godwin on that same play. The official game book is the basis for the entry above; retain the discrepancy for film review. NBC's player note calls the penalty 22 yards, while the game book records 21. We have not independently charted the film.

## 5. Interpretation and assessment

##### Assessment and next observation

**What changed:** our Week 7 expectation of a broader fantasy opportunity did not materialize. Hurst's actual zero and one official target weaken the immediate fantasy case.

**What persisted:** he had meaningful three-receiver-set access and, according to the game book, was involved in a deep shot that drew a first-down penalty as well as a third-and-goal look. These are useful observations, not proof of established quarterback trust or a repeatable target hierarchy.

**What remains unknown:** the single, unusually low-volume game cannot establish Hurst's separation, broader route menu, first-read status, or performance once Egbuka returns. The canceled play requires the source conflict above to be resolved with film.

**Week 8 checkpoint:** Tampa Bay hosts Atlanta on **Sunday, November 1**. Follow Egbuka's concussion/foot practice status and final designation; reports on October 26 describe his availability as uncertain, not a settled game-day outcome. In a more typical passing-volume game, compare Hurst's routes and target earning with Godwin and Johnson; record any additional penalty-nullified targets separately from official stats. Then compare the same role with Egbuka active when that becomes possible. [Team schedule](https://www.buccaneers.com/schedule/2026/) · [October 26 injury report](https://www.rotoballer.com/player-news/emeka-egbuka-questionable-to-play-in-week-8/1976262)

**Current research posture:** keep the investigation open as a developmental watch. The opportunity signal is worth revisiting; this game does not establish a dependable fantasy start.
