### What the zero misses

The 21-yard penalty produced a first down at Tampa Bay's 45. It was recorded as a **no play**, not an official target, catch or receiving yards; it produced no conventional fantasy points.
- **Second quarter, 14:18, third-and-goal at Carolina's 4:** Hurst's one official target was incomplete. The Buccaneers' game recap describes a shallow out with the pass **deflected at the line by Bobby Okereke**, after which Tampa Bay kicked a field goal. This is not evidence of a Hurst drop. ### Assessment and next observation

**What changed:** our Week 7 expectation of a broader fantasy opportunity did not materialize. Hurst's actual zero and one official target weaken the immediate fantasy case.

**What persisted:** he had meaningful three-receiver-set access and, according to the game book, was involved in a deep shot that drew a first-down penalty as well as a third-and-goal look. These are useful observations, not proof of established quarterback trust or a repeatable target hierarchy.

**What remains unknown:** the single, unusually low-volume game cannot establish Hurst's separation, broader route menu, first-read status, or performance once Egbuka returns. The canceled play requires the source conflict above to be resolved with film.
