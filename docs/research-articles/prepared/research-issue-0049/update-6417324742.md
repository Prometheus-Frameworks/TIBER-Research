##### What the outcome can and cannot resolve

The low passing output challenges the anticipated *payoff* of a broad Minnesota passing rebound. The game stayed within one score in its final result; we should not explain the entire passing result as an established comfortable-lead/garbage-time effect without reconstructing the possession-level game state.

The observed Jones result is compatible with our Flores concern. It does **not** establish the actual blitz rate, simulated-pressure frequency, hot-read mechanism or player-specific coverage exposure. Those require appropriately defined charting or representative film, which this update has not performed.

Mason returned and led the rushing counts, but **Jones left with a knee injury**. [#48](https://github.com/Prometheus-Frameworks/TIBER-Research/issues/48) now records the pre/post-exit split; do not infer a healthy 65% Mason role from the full-game headline.
