### How qualified Unit State evidence could have helped tonight
This is a counterfactual about decision support, not a claim it would have selected the better DST.

| Lens | Dallas decision question | Potential help, if qualified and available before kickoff |
|---|---|---|
| Defensive front / pass rush | Is Dallas creating pressure and sack opportunities despite its low recorded sack total? | Distinguish low sack conversion from weak opportunity generation; sacks alone cannot establish pressure. |
| Secondary / coverage | What changes with Porter and Durant absent? | Show the relevant coverage personnel and uncertainty instead of carrying forward an unchanged aggregate defensive assessment. Do not invent a numerical injury penalty. |
| Tampa Bay offensive line | Does Tampa's protection expose its rookie QB to Dallas's particular rush? | Compare opponent protection vulnerability with Dallas's own rush evidence, using supported exposure denominators and current personnel. |
| QB / skill deployment | Does Daniels's mobility or Tampa's rushing/quick-pass approach change the opportunities? | Put rookie turnover risk alongside escape, run and receiving-deployment context; rookie status alone is insufficient. |
| Front × secondary interaction | Could coverage losses let the ball come out sooner, or could pressure compensate for coverage weakness? | Make competing mechanisms explicit. The direction and magnitude are hypotheses until supported, not automatic deductions from an absence. |
| Alternative defenses | Does New England, Chicago, New York or another reported option offer stronger supported mechanisms? | Compare the same lenses, evidence freshness and missingness across the actual shortlist, rather than analyzing only Dallas. |

The practical benefit would be a clearer explanation of **which part of the matchup supports Dallas, which part undermines it, and what remains unknown**. It might strengthen the Dallas lean, favor an alternative, or justify abstaining from a strong preference.

### What remains to be demonstrated
- Qualified event populations, exposure denominators, personnel/identity joins, source coverage and decision-time availability are prerequisites.
- Aggregate PBP outcomes do not by themselves identify causal front-seven versus secondary performance. LB coverage/blitz roles and pressure × coverage overlap functional units.
- #30's first proposed predictive targets are rushing/receiving opportunity, not fantasy DST points. Better opportunity explanation does not automatically establish better sacks/takeaways/TD forecasts or streamer selection.
- Future investigation should compare the simple overall/matchup baseline with added unit evidence, asking whether it improves explanation, changes the supported decision, and ultimately adds held-out selection value. These two cases alone cannot validate that edge.
