##### What LaFleur actually said

Arizona's [October 16 official report](https://www.azcardinals.com/news/jeremiyah-love-ruled-out-against-rams) explicitly attributes Love's smaller-than-expected receiving role to the ankle injury. LaFleur described Love's hard planting/cutting as compromised and connected that limitation to running routes and separating. He stated that Love is intended to have a substantial pass-game role once physically capable.

This supports a **health-contingent receiving-role plan**. It is not evidence that the role has expanded, a guaranteed target count or a return date.

The [October 12 Arizona Sports clip page](https://arizonasports.com/watch/getting-jeremiyah-love-more-involved-in-the-pass-game/01a1328c-6c50-8008-ea2c-7883246bcabb) describes a discussion of using Love's pass-catching to help the offensive line and move the chains. Only its dated page description was inspected; no playable interview/audio or transcript was assessed. Do not convert that description into an independently verified verbatim coaching statement.

## 7. Next checkpoint and closeout

##### Implication and next test

A healthier receiving-capable RB could, in principle, add an underneath answer, sustain possessions or alter defensive attention. It could also divert targets from Wilson. Those are competing possible effects, not established benefits to Wilson.

Keep Love as an explicit context variable in the existing H1-H4 follow-ups:
1. Verify actual availability and workload, rather than treating active status as full health.
2. Record receiving routes/pass-down deployment, targets and credited team target share where available; distinguish them from carries and total snaps.
3. Compare Wilson's route access, target share, conversion and scoring opportunities alongside McBride, Bourne, Brooks and the other TEs.
4. Assess whether broader offensive effectiveness changes, while retaining opponent, protection, game script and personnel as alternative explanations.

A Wilson game played without Love does not test the proposed healthy-Love mechanism. A return with limited receiving deployment is also an incomplete test.

**Hypothesis disposition:** the explicit coaching explanation strengthens the case for health limiting an intended Love receiving role. Its actual effect on Wilson and the offense remains unresolved. Preserve the original Wilson baseline and the existing Tuesday watch cadence.
