##### New official evidence

**Brooks:** Arizona's [October 26 report](https://www.azcardinals.com/news/jalen-brooks-season-ending-injury-creates-another-challenge-for-cardinals) says Jalen Brooks suffered a dislocated wrist during the first quarter, continued playing and underwent emergency surgery Monday, ending his season. LaFleur says the replacement will be **by committee**. This creates a real receiving-rotation opening, without establishing that Wilson inherits the work.

The same report says Jeremiyah Love is expected to return to practice **Wednesday, October 28**. That is a plan, not a completed practice or a Week 8 activation. These two changes could pull opportunity in different directions. Test their actual deployment rather than assuming all vacated WR work transfers to Wilson.

**Snaps:** The [official October 26 snap report](https://www.azcardinals.com/news/defensive-lineman-l-j-collier-still-a-cardinal-still-getting-more-snaps) records 81 offensive snaps: **Wilson 73, McBride 75 and Brooks 52**. Calculated shares are 90.1%, 92.6% and 64.2%. High Wilson on-field participation in this game is established; actual routes, non-route assignments and personnel splits remain unknown. Offensive-snap percentage cannot resolve the earlier route-participation decline.

Brooks' continued play after injury matters: his season-ending absence is primarily a **prospective** change, not a demonstrated explanation for Wilson's Week 7 allocation. His injury could have affected execution; that has not been independently graded.

**Quarterback:** [Arizona's October 26 QB report](https://www.azcardinals.com/news/as-jacoby-brissett-remains-qb1-carson-beck-remains-topic) quotes LaFleur retaining **Brissett as QB1**. Beck had a back-spasm flare-up and dressed as emergency QB against Denver; his next gameday role was not specified. Do not invent a starter change.



##### Role and touchdown opportunity remain distinct

The [ESPN box](https://www.espn.com/nfl/boxscore/_/gameId/401873019), reread today, preserves Wilson's eight targets/4-55-0 against 44 credited team targets and McBride's 13 targets/10-111-1. That is still **18.2%** for Wilson; the extra target over Weeks 5–6 did not establish a return to the Weeks 3–4 concentration. Brooks and Bourne each had six targets; McBride remains central.

Arizona's [October 25 game report](https://www.azcardinals.com/news/broncos-reign-on-cardinals-celebratory-day) and October 26 QB account report **one TD in ten red-zone possessions over the last two games**. This supports a team finishing problem rather than an absence of all scoring trips. It does **not** supply Wilson's individual red-zone/end-zone target ledger or justify an expected-TD estimate. The game report describes pressure; its account is attributed, not a Wilson-specific throw-quality grade.
