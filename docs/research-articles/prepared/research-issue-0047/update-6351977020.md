##### The key original reply

In his [October 20 reply to Jared Smola](https://x.com/MarkDank/status/2112634844543148497), **Mark Dankenbring** reports:

| Game | Wilson present on reported 13-personnel pass plays | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Week 5 vs DET | **14 of 17 — 82.4%** | Most of the reported passing sample; not near-100%. |
| Week 6 at LAR | **100%** | The reply supplies no raw numerator or denominator. |

The original wording is **“played on”**, not an explicit actual-route count. Under the usual definition of 13 personnel, this supports Wilson occupying the lone-WR position on those passing plays. It does not establish that he released into a route on each play, identify every 13-personnel rushing snap, or quantify targets/efficiency from the grouping.

Dankenbring's [parent post](https://x.com/MarkDank/status/2112630638746735014) separately reports overall route participation of **95% before Week 5, then 81% and 80%**, explicitly crediting **SIS**. The reply does not restate its provider or pass-play inclusion rules. Treat these as attributed public reports, not independently reproduced SIS charting.

**Denominator discrepancy remains:** the previously linked Fantasy Points report gives **18 Week 5 13-personnel dropbacks**, whereas this reply gives **17 pass plays**. Different inclusion rules or charting could explain it, but that has not been established. Keep each source's definitions and counts together; do not turn this into 14/18 or label 14 as a verified route numerator.

## 5. Interpretation and assessment

##### What changes in the case?

The reported lone-WR passing role supports the narrower featured-role hypothesis. The overall route-share decline still stands. **The increase in 13 personnel alone does not establish why Wilson lost overall routes**, particularly when he reportedly stayed on the field for all such pass plays in Week 6.

The Puka comparison remains a hypothesis about valuable opportunity in a condensed receiving group; these presence figures do not establish Puka-like target earning or efficiency.

**Next measurement:** obtain the same provider's team dropbacks, Wilson presence, actual routes and targets separately for **11 / 12 / 13 personnel**. Distinguish substitution from blocking or other non-route activity, and obtain the Week 6 raw 13-personnel passing denominator. Until then, keep exact route counts, weekly 12-personnel totals and lone-WR identities on all rushing plays open.
