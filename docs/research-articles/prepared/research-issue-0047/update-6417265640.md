##### Why Heath's percentages differ

His three post-Harrison values are 14.6%, 20.0% and 16.7%. Our preserved weekly credited-target values are 15.2%, 20.6% and 18.2%.

The reported values numerically match targets divided by **all team pass attempts**: 7/48, 7/35 and 8/48. Week 5's 48 includes Wilson's trick-play attempt, already documented in the original issue. This is a denominator comparison, not proof of every Fantasy Points charting inclusion rule. **Do not splice these values into our existing ledger or call the convention difference a football disagreement.**

The thread also calls Brissett's Week 7 sample **48 dropbacks**. The box records **48 pass attempts plus three sacks**, already at least 51 pass/sack dropbacks before identifying any scramble dropbacks. We cannot use 48 as a reconciled full-game dropback denominator or derive route participation from it.

McBride's displayed **27.1 points** matches **full PPR** from 10 catches/111 yards/1 TD; the same receiving line is 17.1 without reception credit. The intake notes' broad suggestion of standard scoring should not govern our comparisons.
