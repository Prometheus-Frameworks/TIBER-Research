### Accuracy beyond completion percentage

The inspected [NFL Next Gen Stats 2026 regular-season passing table](https://nextgenstats.nfl.com/stats/passing/2026/REG/all) lists Brissett at **66.7% completed versus 60.8% expected: +5.9 percentage points over expectation**, across 234 attempts. It also lists 7.2 average intended air yards, 4.8 average completed air yards and 2.59 seconds time to throw.

**Interpretation:** this model-adjusted completion evidence is favorable; the aggregate data does not support diagnosing him simply as an inaccurate passer. CPOE is not a pure placement grade, proof of correct decisions, or a Wilson-specific catchability assessment. Low yards per attempt and mixed game results remain separate concerns. Do not infer that favorable aggregate accuracy means every receiver's targets have been well placed.

PFR's separate charting lists 82.2% on target and 13.8% bad throws, with its own definitions/adjusted denominator. PFR lists 6.9 intended air yards per attempt while NGS lists 7.2. Retain those as source-specific measurements; do not merge them into one charting ledger or claim independent agreement on every field.

### Beck's promising sample and current readiness limits

Arizona's [August 6 preseason account](https://www.azcardinals.com/news/for-starters-carson-beck-impressive-in-nfl-preseason-debut) confirms **15 of 19 for 188 yards and one touchdown** against Carolina in the Hall of Fame game. LaFleur praised his operation and decisions. The same account notes that key players on both teams were absent; it is one preseason performance rather than a regular-season upgrade estimate.

The latest [official practice report actually inspected](https://www.azcardinals.com/news/injury-report-week-7-vs-broncos) is **Wednesday, October 21**: Beck was limited with a back injury, and the account says he was not throwing during the open portion. This does not establish his Thursday status or a return timetable.

### Wilson-study interpretation and next observations

Brissett's aggregate accuracy and overall sack rate do not by themselves create a strong immediate benching argument. An eventual rookie evaluation is nevertheless a material ROS possibility supported by attributed reporting, even if Brissett continues as the preferred current starter. A future Beck start is not automatically an improvement for Wilson.

At existing follow-ups, verify QB health, listed order, activation, actual first-team work where reported, coaching selection statements and confirmed transactions. If a change occurs, compare Wilson/McBride/Love deployment, target distribution, catchable opportunity where charted and team efficiency without assuming the QB alone caused the difference. Keep opponent, protection, availability, personnel and game script in view.

**Disposition:** accuracy evidence favorable; passing efficiency remains limited; immediate change unannounced; later-season Beck evaluation remains a substantive uncertainty. No new automation, transaction or expanded research lane is created.
