**October 22, 2026 · America/Toronto**

Joe asked to finish this research pass by naming the remaining unknowns, especially **coverage and the defenses Wilson has faced**, before submitting a final hypothesis or judgment. The register below records that boundary. A missing witness limits a particular claim; it is neither negative evidence nor a requirement to investigate every possible variable indefinitely.

### Known opponents, unmeasured matchup context

The original receiving ledger identifies **W1 LAC, W2 SEA, W3 SF, W4 NYG, W5 DET and W6 LAR**. The missing witness is not who Arizona played. It is the qualified defensive context and Wilson's actual exposure in those games.

A team's D/ST fantasy score, record or broad reputation cannot stand in for its ability to limit Wilson's receiving opportunities. Likewise, a defense's aggregate man/zone tendency is not proof of the coverage on Wilson's routes.
