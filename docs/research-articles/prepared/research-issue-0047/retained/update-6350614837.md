**Update kind:** Arizona offensive-context amendment; no new game result.

### What LaFleur actually said

Arizona's [October 16 official report](https://www.azcardinals.com/news/jeremiyah-love-ruled-out-against-rams) explicitly attributes Love's smaller-than-expected receiving role to the ankle injury. LaFleur described Love's hard planting/cutting as compromised and connected that limitation to running routes and separating. He stated that Love is intended to have a substantial pass-game role once physically capable.

This supports a **health-contingent receiving-role plan**. It is not evidence that the role has expanded, a guaranteed target count or a return date.
