## 5. Interpretation and assessment

##### Independent research interpretation

The carry concentration can be strong while the fantasy result is modest. This game supports a contingent rushing lead **with Montgomery absent**, while very limited receiving contribution and no TD constrained output. It does not establish a healthy-Montgomery takeover, prove the runner alone caused low efficiency, or define a permanent 9-point ceiling.

Heath's five career games with 18+ carries are a selected descriptive sample. They are useful leads for examining rushing access, receiving opportunity and scoring environment, not a calibrated forward forecast or a causal test that volume cannot help him.
