#### Boston / Vele

**Question:** Does Vele's steady opportunity and reported slot/man/blitz fit outweigh Boston's downfield production in half-PPR?

**Case for Vele**

- At least six targets in every game through Week 5 supports an established workload.
- His slot deployment creates a plausible connection to where the Giants attract passing targets.
- A quicker passing response to pressure could benefit Vele. Chris Allen's matchup discussion describes a related Shough/Vele mechanism.

**Case for Boston**

- Greater receiving yardage and scoring through Week 5 have outweighed his lower catch volume.
- His reported performance against two-high provides a favourable coverage argument against Baltimore.
- Half-PPR gives Vele's additional receptions less weight than full PPR would.

**Counterargument and uncertainty:** a high share of targets directed to the slot identifies where opponents throw. It does not establish poor defensive efficiency. FantasyPros reports relatively low fantasy production per slot opportunity against New York. The expected volume and efficiency of those opportunities must be assessed separately.

**Current assessment:** Boston narrowly; Vele remains a reasonable alternative.

**Reconsider if:** Boston is limited, deployment or quarterback availability changes, Vele's expected involvement increases materially, or game conditions change the comparison.



#### Boston / Metcalf

**Question:** How should Boston's recent production be weighed against Metcalf's opportunity in Pittsburgh's depleted receiver room?

**Case for Boston:** recent receiving production, a favourable reported Baltimore coverage matchup, and clearance from his foot designation Friday.

**Case for Metcalf:** missing Pittsburgh receivers create available receiving work. His reported positive performance against single-high also fits Tampa's frequent use of those looks.

**Counterargument and uncertainty:** Metcalf was questionable with an illness. Being active would not itself establish an unrestricted workload. Receiver absences support opportunity, but the allocation of that opportunity remains uncertain.

**Current assessment:** Boston narrowly while Metcalf's illness adds uncertainty; a healthy Metcalf makes this close.

**Reconsider if:** Metcalf's availability and expected workload improve, or Boston develops a supported limitation. A Metcalf absence also affects the tight-end comparison below.



#### Pitts / Freiermuth

**Question:** Does Pitts' improving involvement and reported coverage fit outweigh Freiermuth's recent workload and Pittsburgh's receiver absences?

**Case for Freiermuth:** recent targets, increased route participation and a depleted receiver room support continued involvement.

**Case for Pitts:** improving recent involvement and the alignment/man/Cover 2 fit described in DataRoma's analysis provide a plausible matchup advantage.

**Counterarguments**

- Tampa's frequent Cover 3 / single-high deployment intersects with Freiermuth's reported weaker target earning and efficiency against those looks.
- Darnell Washington is another possible recipient of Pittsburgh's available receiving work; increased tight-end opportunity need not all flow to Freiermuth.
- Pitts' route participation has been volatile, and his recent target total remains modest.

**Assessment revision:** the initial modest Freiermuth preference gave greater weight to recent workload and receiver absences. Further consideration of the specific coverage mismatch and Washington's role shifted the assessment to a small conditional Pitts lean. Zain's reported splits reinforce an already identified concern; another analyst's report is not automatically an independent dataset.

**Current assessment:** Pitts slightly with Metcalf playing; Freiermuth if Metcalf is out. Confidence remains limited.

**Analytical limits:** Cover 3 overlaps single-high, and blitz overlaps coverage classifications. Correlated splits should not be added as independent boosts or penalties. Historical splits can reflect different quarterbacks, personnel and roles. Route participation and target earning are separate.
