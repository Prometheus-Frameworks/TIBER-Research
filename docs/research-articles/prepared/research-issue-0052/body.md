#### Pregame evidence and working interpretation

- The Rams' October 23 report lists Puka **questionable** with a calf injury: limited Thursday, DNP Friday. Davante Adams is already sidelined; the team reports a 6–10-week absence.
- Mumpfield's Week 3 line against Denver was **8 targets, 4 receptions, 93 yards, 1 TD**: 17.3 half-PPR / 19.3 PPR points. Margin lists that as **WR11 in half-PPR**; do not carry that ranking across scoring formats.
- Puka missed that game; Adams played and drew substantial defensive attention. Mumpfield caught consecutive passes for 21, 9 and 48 yards on one late fourth-quarter drive, finishing with an out-and-up touchdown. That sequence supports timing and route setup, while the concentration of production limits what one fantasy finish establishes.
- Public qualitative separation context: Scott Barrett's September 30 post reported Mumpfield seventh in Average Separation Score since the start of 2025. The post does not specify the qualifying route minimum or numerical score. The October 20 Fantasy Points Data chart also prompted this investigation. These are dated external observations, not an independently reproduced TIBER ranking or a licensed dataset.
- Fantasy Points describes ASS as route-level separation charting with coverage, alignment and route splits. Strong separation alone does not establish target volume.
- The October 21 Fantasy Footballers report lists **17 targets on 112 routes (15.2% TPRR)** through Week 6. Preserve the provider and denominator when comparing future reports.
- The Rams' 2025 draft profile emphasized route precision and outside/slot versatility. WR coach Eric Yarber praised his releases and separation during June 2025 OTAs. Working interpretation: a receiver whose case rests on route pacing, timing and creating space at breaks, with consistent NFL target earning still unresolved.
- Whittington, Atwell, other receivers, tight ends and backs can share the vacated opportunity. Missing stars do not mechanically transfer their targets to Mumpfield.

**Pregame judgment, not a projection:** if Puka sits, Mumpfield is a plausible but volatile WR3/flex candidate, particularly in deeper leagues. If Puka plays, the evidence supports a deeper-league option pending clearer role expansion. Adams' absence creates a potential runway beyond this single game.
