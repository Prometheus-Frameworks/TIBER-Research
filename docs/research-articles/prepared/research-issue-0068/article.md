**Central question:** When McConkey is available, does he regain enough sustained route access and target involvement in the 2026 Chargers offense to produce a reliable PPR role, or do the passing environment and competition limit him even after his health improves?

We are interested in a rebound, but that is a working possibility, not a predetermined conclusion. An injury-affected sample can obscure the answer in either direction: it is not proof of declining ability, and an early productive game does not guarantee a recovery.

### Hypotheses and decision signals

- **Rebound:** recovery is followed by sustained field access and a meaningful, repeatable share of targets. Stronger support would be several complete games with high route participation and useful target concentration—not one long TD alone.
- **Available but crowded:** he returns to a substantial route load, yet target concentration stays modest while Harris, Johnston or other targets retain roles. A strong fantasy game could still result from an efficient play.
- **Environment constraint:** his opportunity is reasonable but the offense's passing volume, protection or scoring chances limit fantasy outcomes. Test the team-level conditions rather than attributing everything to the player.
- **Unresolved:** missed games, early exits, conflicting route data or too few complete appearances prevent a confident comparison.

As descriptive prompts, consider whether **multiple complete games** show roughly 75%+ route participation and around 20%+ target share; conversely, repeated substantial route participation with a much lower share would merit investigating target competition and deployment. These are heuristic prompts, not validated thresholds, automatic grades, or guarantees. Compare like-for-like sources, sample sizes and contexts; do not score missed games as ordinary low-usage games.

At the final checkpoint, report: (a) availability, (b) observed role, (c) outcomes, (d) which hypotheses gained or lost support, (e) what remains unknown, and (f) the practical fantasy implication for PPR and survival formats, preserving uncertainty and roster-specific alternatives.
