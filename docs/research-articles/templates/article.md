# [Subject] — [window and research purpose]

Header instructions: replace unknown/null only with sourced values. Record kind
can be matchup preview, player/team study, comparison, experiment, conceptual note
or external-reading note. Origin distinguishes original, historical format copy,
dated update and retrospective research. Authoring time is not evidence freshness.
Link a game manifest where one exists. Preserve any real external acceptance
receipt without claiming this article grants it. Remove these instructions in the
completed article. This is a publishing template, not a validated data contract.

## 1. Summary

State the most useful current finding or question and its principal condition.
For a formatting copy, identify the historical state and explicitly say that no
new evidence or review was performed. Avoid presenting an old finding as current.

## 2. Question and scope

What are we trying to distinguish, why does it matter, and what is the window?
List selected question IDs and any deferred questions/reasons. Retain original IDs.
Label new IDs for legacy records as retrospective organizational mappings.

## 3. Baseline and alternatives

Preserve the original working hypothesis, the strongest credible alternative,
source-backed starting state, relevant personnel/regime and evidence that could
distinguish the explanations. Separate the operator's stated position from agent
interpretation and exploratory questions. Preserve pregame uncertainty.

## 4. Evidence by question

Repeat a separate card for every material selected/linked question:

- Question ID and related IDs:
- Observation / attributed report / calculation / interpretation (label each):
- Exact source/artifact locator; provider and shared source lineage:
- Publication/update time, retrieval time and latest event/sample covered:
- Metric definition, numerator, denominator, exclusions, personnel and game window:
- Supporting passage/calculation or scoped film/play locator:
- Contrary evidence, conflicting definitions and observed access failures:
- Remaining unknowns and whether the original test was observable:

Preserve specialized matchup selection/evidence-card fields. A table or readable
summary may accompany the cards, not erase distinct evidence requirements.

## 5. Interpretation and assessment

Assess each original question within its scope. Distinguish pending, supported,
challenged, mixed or unresolved where appropriate, with reasoning and limitations.
Retain historical statuses rather than manufacturing a new assessment during
formatting. Keep role, exposure, opportunity, execution and fantasy points separate.
Give observed-state and prospective-implication confidence separately if supported;
no invented probability or numeric confidence score.

## 6. Unknowns and revision triggers

What remains unobserved or disputed? What specific evidence or condition would
change the view? Which causal or next-game conclusions cannot be established?
New postgame discoveries are explicitly retrospective, not original predictions.

## 7. Next checkpoint and closeout

Record the actual owner, next checkpoint, observation endpoint and evidence
deadline, or mark unknown/proposed. Name the action needed for closure, deferral
or a linked successor. Preserve multiweek study windows. A proposed checkpoint
is not a confirmed schedule. A complete account can have unresolved findings.

Potential reuse identifies specific statements, source revision, destination,
intended use, validity conditions and additional acceptance requirements. The
article itself grants no source admission or consumer activation.

## 8. Sources, review and amendment history

Retain original and supplemental sources, shared lineage and material source
limitations. Identify author checks and independent review separately, with exact
reviewed revision and receipt or explicit pending/unknown. Preserve existing
publication and merge state without upgrading it during preparation.

For each amendment: actual authoring time/timezone, affected IDs, previous view,
new evidence and sample/cutoff, revised or unchanged interpretation, open limits,
and links to the earlier record. Preserve original bodies and cutoffs. A historical
format copy states when it was prepared and exactly which source versions it maps.
