# Codex Research Arena v1

Effective October 20, 2026 at 08:22:23 America/Toronto
(`2026-10-20T12:22:23Z`). The prospective deadline change first applies to the
October 25, 2026 Sunday slate. This document supplements the retained
[v0 experiment](EXPERIMENT.md); it does not rewrite earlier choices, evidence,
deadlines or results.

## Standing assignment

Investigate the current football slate, choose worthwhile questions, and build
a defensible hypothetical lineup within the arena's constraints. Explain the
useful insights, how they affect the whole team, and what remains uncertain.
Preserve expectations that can be examined after the games, and carry useful
findings into the next research pass.

Codex chooses its research priorities without waiting for a new player question.
It may discover candidates outside the current lineup, challenge an earlier
interpretation, follow a promising football question, or decide that the existing
team remains justified. There is no quota of questions, discoveries, revisions
or lessons. Allocate effort to the uncertainties that matter and stop when the
bounded pass has produced a useful answer or an explicit evidence limit.

The lineup makes the investigation concrete. The continuing research record
lets an observer understand and learn from the interpretation. Financial profit,
contest entry and roster transactions remain outside the arena. Codex is the
only research agent in this version; no Claude assignment or coordination loop
is activated.

## Each research pass

1. Read this protocol, the latest slate and lineup record, relevant unresolved
   questions, any existing freeze, and prior outcome reviews. Establish which
   retained lessons remain applicable.
2. Survey material changes and choose the football questions worth investigating.
   Explain briefly why each selected question matters. A question may concern a
   player outside the current build or an interaction across several positions.
3. Investigate using attributable current sources and eligible retained evidence.
   Consider competing explanations and evidence that challenges the preferred
   interpretation. Preserve source dates, relevant windows and missing data.
4. Keep or revise the hypothetical team. Explain the salary tradeoff and the
   effect on the whole lineup. A supported hold is a successful decision. Label
   the review as self-review unless a separate review actually occurred.
5. Record the useful finding, its implication, remaining uncertainty, and the
   observation that would change the view. Write concise public explanations;
   do not expose private internal deliberation.
6. Archive a dated notebook and its journal entry, update the display feed, and
   verify readback through the existing publication path. Distinguish successful
   research, successful archival, and successful display publication.

An unavailable slate, price, status or source produces an honest partial result.
Preserve the useful work and the last valid lineup; do not fill gaps with invented
facts. Resource, model and cost details are recorded only when actually observed.
Material operator input is identified so independently chosen work remains
distinguishable from a prompted follow-up.

## Expectations and outcomes

For the assumptions that matter, record prospective football expectations before
the deadline: what role or game condition is expected, what observation would
support or challenge it, and what would trigger a lineup reconsideration.
Use measurable thresholds when supported; avoid invented probabilities or
precision. Missing evidence may make an expectation unassessable.

The final freeze binds the chosen lineup, original salary snapshot, relevant
expectations and actual cutoff through immutable notebook references. An already
valid freeze survives retries. Later news and results receive separate entries;
they cannot change the scored selection. Preserve a late or partial freeze
honestly, without reconstructing a pregame decision from later information.

After the slate, compare the observed roles and conditions with those expectations
as well as calculating the verified fantasy points. State which parts were
supported, contradicted or left unassessable. A touchdown or a low score alone
does not settle a role explanation. Preserve provisional scoring, misses and
later corrections.

Carry a useful lesson forward with its source, limits and implication for a
future investigation. A single game supports a provisional learning note, not
a universal rule. Learning here means retained evidence and improved research
practice that later sessions can inspect.

## Journal and continuity

The [feed convention](SITE_FEED.md) adds an optional, append-only research journal
linked to the existing lineup snapshots. Each entry carries its own timestamp
and distinguishes scheduled research, interactive research, later annotation
and outcome review. A later explanation of an earlier decision must say when
it was added and identify the archived record on which it is based.

Retain journal records as `journal/<date-or-actual-UTC-timestamp>.md` with
companion JSON in this notebook folder. Use a fresh timestamped filename for
additional same-day work; do not replace an existing entry. The Site feed is
the display copy of those retained entries.

Do not reconstruct historical self-chosen questions, expectations or evidence
times. Future questions can be carried forward from an old record while clearly
remaining new questions. A display refresh or journal annotation is not a new
football investigation.

Preserve all existing snapshot identities, selections, original salaries,
projection snapshots, rationales and immutable notebooks. Reuse the two existing
tasks and the stable `codex/weekly-dfs-feed` branch. Routine runs update data
through the current guarded notebook/feed procedure; they do not redeploy the
Site or create another schedule.

## Scope retained from v0

The source, temporal-integrity, public-record, scoring and publication boundaries
in [EXPERIMENT.md](EXPERIMENT.md) remain in effect except for the prospective
deadline and research-initiative additions above. Current qualified ROP/TTS
evidence can inform a pass within its actual coverage; unavailable evidence stays
unavailable and descriptive data is not presented as a production forecast.

Scheduled writes remain limited to `docs/weekly-dfs/codex/` through documentation
branches/PRs and the existing stable display feed. There are no automatic merges,
producer runs, paid-provider purchases, credential changes, real-world fantasy
actions or new integrations. Keep private account, league and personal context
out of public records. These remain conversational research notes, separate from
the repository's governed Research-run machinery.
