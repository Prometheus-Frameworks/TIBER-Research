**Why I decline:**

   - After those reports, the market still puts Arizona 5.5 points below Las Vegas. Arizona remains the lowest implied team total I can see on the slate.
   - For a DST, points allowed is the largest scoring component on DraftKings.
   - A median near 15.5 sits mid-bracket in 14–20, which scores +1, with real chances of 7–13 (+4).
   - A median near 21 sits right at the 20/21 boundary, so it is roughly a coin flip between +1 and 0.
   - Losing three Seattle front players is real, but I cannot show that it outweighs a 5.5-point gap in opponent scoring that was set after the market saw it.
2. **Sack and turnover chances do not clearly favor SF.**
   - Bosa returning helps SF. Seattle losing Williams, Nwosu and Pili hurts Seattle.
   - But Arizona is also missing two starting offensive linemen.
   - Both teams are similar favorites (−8.5 and −9.5), so the game script should be about the same.
   - I see no evidence that nets out in SF's favor. I did not invent a pressure-rate estimate.
3. **The $200 saving buys nothing.** The candidate leaves $800 unspent and no alternative uses it. The swap trades a defense two inputs prefer for cash that sits idle.
4. **The single external model agrees.** It has SEA 7.1 vs SF 6.5, and Codex records the change as a deliberate −0.6 departure from that estimate. DraftKings' season averages (7.4 vs 6.0) also lean Seattle, but they are history, not projections, and were earned partly with the now-absent front.

**What would change my mind:**
