## Thesis

The lineup is built on **San Francisco at Dallas**, the slate's outlier game, and on four cheap roles that injuries opened in Week 9.

SF@DAL is four points above the next total. HOU@CLE is the lowest on the slate.

- **Prescott and Lamb.** Dallas is a 4.5-point home underdog in a 55.5 game, about 25.5 implied points with a trailing script. Dallas's receiver room is thin: Pickens (hamstring) and Turpin (rib) were out in Week 9, Mingo left with an ankle injury, and Ferguson left with a knee injury. Lamb had 10 catches for 195 yards and a touchdown with full route participation.
- **McCaffrey and Watkins** are the San Francisco side, about 30 implied points. McCaffrey's Week 9 usage was reported as normal outside garbage time. Watkins played Z, had 4 catches for 86 yards and a touchdown, and Yahoo calls Evans (Achilles) unlikely for Week 10 with Kirk on IR.
- **Kaleb Johnson.** Jacobs fractured a finger and is reported out up to three games after surgery. Lloyd missed Week 9 with a knee injury. Johnson closed that game and is reported as the starter, at $4,600 on a 3.5-point home favorite.
- **Mumpfield.** Nacua is reported heading to IR and Adams is on IR. Mumpfield had 11 catches for 162 yards and a touchdown in Week 9 while Nacua was still playing. He costs $4,000 on a 7.5-point favorite.
- **Marks and the Houston DST** are a deliberate same-team pair. Montgomery has missed three straight games, and Marks had 66% of snaps and 18 carries. Houston is a road favorite in the lowest-total game. Cleveland started Sanders with Watson (concussion) inactive.
- **Kelce.** His route participation and target rate were reported as normal. Kansas City's receiver room is a five-man committee with Worthy (hamstring) ruled out mid-game and Rice under 67% routes in two straight games.

How the Week 9 lessons were used:
- *Targets, catches and points differ.* Mumpfield and Watkins are picked on role vacancy, but I only have catch lines for them, not route or target shares. That is flagged below.
- *Role misses can hide behind touchdowns.* Watkins's line included a touchdown on four targets. I count it as a weaker role signal than Mumpfield's eleven catches.
- *Points allowed alone does not settle a DST score.* The Houston case rests on a backup or returning-from-concussion quarterback and a favorite script, not only on the 39.5 total.

## Strongest counterargument

1. **Four of nine come from one game, and that game's total is already the public number.** If SF@DAL lands in the mid-40s, Prescott, Lamb, McCaffrey and Watkins fail together. San Francisco as a 4.5-point favorite can also control the game on the ground, which helps McCaffrey and hurts Watkins.
2. **Three cheap roles are one news item from disappearing.** Samuel is "nearing a return" (Watkins). Lloyd's availability is unclear, and Johnson's starter label comes from one waiver article (Johnson). Montgomery could return (Marks). I have no practice report for any of them, because none exists yet this week.
3. **Mumpfield's big game came with Nacua on the field.** Without Nacua he should draw better coverage, and Atwell ($3,700) had 99 yards in the same game. The Rams as a 7.5-point favorite may not need to throw.
4. **Kelce depends on Mahomes's right arm.** PFF reports an arm injury late in the second quarter of Week 9, and Yahoo lists it as an elbow. He finished the game, and I found no follow-up report.
5. **Marks is a low-catch back** (one reception in Week 9) in a 39.5-total game. On full-PPR scoring he needs a touchdown.

The market sources also disagree. VegasInsider (updated Nov 9, 22:43) shows LAR −9.5 with a 40.5 total and lists several home and away teams reversed. I used the Fox table because its home and away sides match the NFL schedule.
