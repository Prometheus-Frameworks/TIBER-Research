### 5. Interpretation and assessment

- **H1 — sustained featured role: strengthened, persistence unresolved.** A 36.6% share is direct target-concentration evidence. It does not establish sustained route access or target earning per route.
- **H2 — stable leading WR with constrained ceiling: weakened for this game.** Wilson led all Arizona receivers in targets and exceeded the recent seven/eight-target pattern. A stable leading-WR role itself remains compatible; its supposed ceiling is less secure.
- **H3 — attention/passing execution suppressing conversion: unresolved.** The QB-segment split makes execution worth testing, but target difficulty, protection, coverage and game state were not held constant. We cannot allocate blame or credit from these outcomes alone.
- **H4 — scoring-opportunity changes: unresolved longitudinally.** Four individual red-zone targets establish meaningful Week 8 chances, separately from the TD. Their change versus the earlier weeks is still unknown; one score does not establish a new scoring rate or that he was “due.”

**Role assessment:** A real concentration rebound with substantial context qualifications. **Touchdown assessment:** A realized TD supported by multiple observed scoring chances, with no established season-long opportunity trend or expected-TD estimate.
