## Choosing the next question

Start with what the player has already demonstrated. Name the remaining uncertainty, the observation that could distinguish two explanations, and when it becomes possible to check. A team-level look can surface an unstudied player when personnel, opportunity or usage changes. Prefer a question about that change to another recap of a familiar player.

When Week 11 ends, reconcile new issues, amendments in older studies, and due entries in the resolution log. Link the dated answer where one exists. For a continuing question, give its next observable test. For a question with little new to learn, preserve the last assessment and identify a reason to reopen. Record any weeks with missing evidence explicitly.

At season’s end, compare cases where the team record caught a change with cases first spotted elsewhere. Revisit missed changes and repeated checks that yielded little additional evidence. Use those examples to choose next season’s follow-up cadence.
