# Kicker / DST streaming: preparation packet

Prepared October 1, 2026 for [Research #25](https://github.com/Prometheus-Frameworks/TIBER-Research/issues/25). Coordination: [Ops #92](https://github.com/Prometheus-Frameworks/TIBER-Ops/issues/92). Status: **design and source readiness only; no empirical run or weekly ranking activated**.

## Decision in brief

Existing work gives us a useful starting point for describing team activity, but does not yet establish a tested streaming edge. The legacy DST tool is implemented and mounted in the full Fantasy source tree; its fixed profiles, missing provenance clocks and generic score-to-points conversion are not a qualified 2026 forecasting baseline. Earlier reviewed W1–W2 TTS evidence remains useful for bounded retrospective description even when newer work is blocked. It does not establish what was knowable before a historical waiver deadline.

The smallest defensible next empirical step is a separately activated, retained-input Carolina diagnostic, after exact artifact custody and purpose acceptance are checked. A predictive replay needs additional historical availability witnesses. Kickers need separate identity, attempt, distance and scoring-event support. Missing replay inputs should reduce the study, not be filled with current information.

## Reporting vocabulary

- **Observed:** directly read records or source fields, with exact revision and observation time
- **Derived:** reproducible arithmetic from named eligible inputs, never a new observed fact
- **External consensus:** attributed, dated third-party ranking or market snapshot; no such baseline is qualified here
- **Interpretation:** researcher explanation or hypothesis, including competing explanations
- **Not verified in this preparation:** not inspected or not authenticated here; not equivalent to unavailable everywhere
- **Unavailable within the inspected binding:** the inspected contract lacks a field/witness; do not infer a value
- **Blocked:** a specific next action cannot meet its gate yet; does not erase earlier usable work

Any later Fantasy/TEAM presentation requires its own identity, purpose, source-use and serving review. This packet changes no user-facing surface and makes no roster recommendation or transaction.
