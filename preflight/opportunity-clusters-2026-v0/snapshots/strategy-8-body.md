## Summary

Capture the operator reasoning and draft dialogue from a collaborative 12-team PPR Sleeper mock draft. The objective is not to preserve the roster itself, but the decision framework that emerged while drafting.

## Status

Preseason working artifact. Observations should be revisited before the live draft and audited after the 2026 season.

## Key discoveries

- Board Geometry emerged as a draft-state concept rather than a player-ranking concept.
- Opportunity Clusters proved more useful than isolated player rankings once positional tiers flattened.
- Pivot Points were identified by changes in board state rather than predetermined rounds.
- Survivability of tiers influenced decisions more than positional need.
- Draft decisions should account for downstream resources including FAAB, waivers and trades.
- The draft is one acquisition window rather than the entire season.

## Draft methodology

- Independent TIBER operator selected each pick from the live board.
- Human operator graded convergence using trophy/green/yellow markers.
- Disagreements were treated as research opportunities rather than mistakes.
- Focus remained on reasoning, not ADP conformity.

## Success criteria

Produce a reusable strategy framework that helps operators reason about drafts without prescribing a single correct pick, while preserving human agency and remaining grounded in observable evidence.
