## Status
Preseason operator hypothesis. Observations derived from repeated Sleeper mock drafts under the current market environment. These are working hypotheses to revisit after the 2026 season.

## Objective
Capture operator reasoning before outcomes are known.

## Concepts observed
- Board Geometry
- Opportunity Clusters
- Pivot Points
- Survivability of tiers
- Draft as one acquisition window (FAAB, waivers and trades included)
- Draft mispriced opportunity rather than positional need

## Candidate operator laws (hypotheses)
1. Draft opportunity before positional need.
2. Opportunity clusters are often more important than precise player rankings.
3. The board determines when to pivot, not the pre-draft plan.
4. Draft decisions should consider downstream resource consumption (FAAB, trades, roster flexibility).
5. Board geometry is dynamic and specific to the current market.

## Audit after season
- Which observations held?
- Which failed?
- Did pivot points occur where expected?
- Did opportunity clusters outperform isolated rankings?
- How did live drafts differ from Sleeper mocks?
